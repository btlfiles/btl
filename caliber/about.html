About Caliber Paint

The standard is the whole business.

Caliber Paint started in Saratoga Springs in 2013 with a simple idea: do the job like it's your own house, and the next job takes care of itself. Thirteen years later, most of our new customers still come from past ones. That only happens when the work holds up.

Prep is 90% of the job

Putty, caulk, mask, prime. It's the part nobody photographs and the reason our paint jobs still look right when your neighbor's is peeling. If a budget can't cover doing it right, we'll say so and step aside rather than skip what matters.

Our people, not subs

Everyone on your job works directly for us: hired, background-checked, and trained to our standard. When you let a painter into your home, you should know exactly who's showing up. With us, you do.

Straight answers

We'll tell you when your walls need one coat instead of two, when a repaint can wait a year, and when a cheap bid you got is going to cost you more. Even when it costs us the job.

  • Licensed & insured in Utah
  • Every employee background-checked, no subcontractors
