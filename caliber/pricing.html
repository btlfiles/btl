Pricing

What painting actually costs. In writing. On the internet.

Almost no painter publishes prices. We get why: it's easier to size up a customer in person. We'd rather you walk into your estimate knowing what quality work costs, so nobody can play games with you, including us.

Ranges reflect typical jobs across our service area, Provo to Park City to Kaysville. Every house is different; your written bid is fixed once we've seen the job.

Why the lowest bid is low

When a bid comes in thousands under everyone else, the money didn't come from nowhere. It came out of prep hours, paint quality, insurance, or the wage that attracts painters who know what they're doing. The most expensive paint job you'll ever buy is the cheap one you pay for twice.

What moves a bid down

  • Empty rooms or cleared walls before we arrive
  • Flexible timing, so we can slot you where our schedule has gaps
  • Bundling: whole-home beats room-by-room, cabinets + interior beats separate trips
  • Good existing condition, since prep is most of the labor
