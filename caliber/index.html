Straight Talk

Why one painter bids $3,500 and another bids $5,500 for the "same" job.

Because it isn't the same job. Here's where the cheap bid saves the money:

Prep

Paint is only as good as what's under it. Washing, sanding, priming, and caulking are most of the labor. The low bid skips most of it. You find out in 18 months.

Materials

Contractor-grade paint sprayed thin looks fine on day one. We spec Sherwin-Williams products matched to each surface, apply them at full coverage, and name every product in your written bid.

The crew

Our painters are our employees: trained to our standard, on our payroll. Not day labor, not whoever answered a text this morning.

A good bid shows you exactly where the money goes: the prep, the products, the people. That's what ours do, in writing, before you commit to anything.

Questions We Get

Fair questions. Honest answers.

Why aren't you the cheapest bid?

Because we do the prep the cheap bid skips, use materials at full coverage, and pay experienced employees instead of day labor. Our bids reflect what the job actually takes to do right. And if a budget can't cover doing it right, we'll say so up front and point you in a better direction, not take your money and quietly cut corners.

How soon can you start?

Tell us your timeline and we'll give you a straight answer about whether we can hit it. No overpromising to win the job. And once you're on the schedule, we show up when we said we would.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, licensed and insured in Utah. And every person who steps on your property has passed a background check. We're one of the only painters in the state who can say that.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes, a 2-year written workmanship warranty on every job. And we'll be straight with you about why it's 2 years and not 7: kitchens live hard lives, and we'd rather promise 2 years and mean every day of it than promise 7 and lawyer our way out. But the real policy is simpler than any warranty: if something's not right, we make it right, whatever that takes. In 13 years that reputation is the reason our customers do our advertising for us.

Do you use subcontractors?

No. Everyone on your job works directly for us: our hires, our training, our standard. We will never hand your house to a crew we found last week.

Do you work with builders?

Yes, and we'd like to work with more. Custom builders and remodelers keep us for the finish work because our crews hit schedules and don't create punch-list items. Send plans for a bid, or call and talk to Beau directly.

Can we live in the house while you work?

Almost everyone does. We work in phases, seal off the work area, and leave your home usable every night. Whole-home interiors typically run 3 to 5 days, single rooms are usually done in one, and we'll map the order of rooms around your life, not ours.

What paint do you actually use?

Sherwin-Williams, specced to the job: one line for high-traffic walls, another for cabinets, another for trim. Your written bid names the exact product going on every surface, so you can look it up yourself. What we never do is quote "paint" as a mystery line item.

Do you ask for money up front?

You'll never be asked to pay in full before the work is done. Payment terms are spelled out in your written bid before we start, no surprises at the end. If a painter wants the whole job paid up front, that's your cue to keep shopping.
