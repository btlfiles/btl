Cabinets & Specialty Finishes

Cabinets refinished to a factory-smooth sprayed finish.

The single highest-impact update in your house. We degrease, sand, prime, and spray your existing cabinets with cabinet-grade coatings, not wall paint with a brush. And for the work most painters won't attempt, we do wood-grain and decorative finishes too.

What's included. Every time.

  • Doors and drawers removed and sprayed flat for a factory finish
  • Full degrease and sand so the finish actually bonds
  • Bonding primer plus Sherwin-Williams cabinet-grade enamel, not wall paint
  • Boxes masked and sprayed in place, and you keep using your kitchen most of the week
  • Hardware removed and reinstalled, or swapped if you're upgrading
  • Durable finish made for fingerprints, cooking grease, and kids
